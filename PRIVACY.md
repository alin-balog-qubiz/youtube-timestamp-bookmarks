# Privacy Policy — YouTube Bookmarks

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

YouTube Bookmarks is a Chrome extension for saving and revisiting timestamps in YouTube videos. It stores the information needed for those features in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

## Information the extension handles

After you agree to the in-extension privacy notice, the extension reads the current YouTube video's ID and displayed title, and reads the playback position when you use the player’s save control. It stores:

- YouTube video IDs and displayed titles;
- saved timestamps and their creation dates;
- optional bookmark names and color overrides;
- your appearance, marker-visibility, and default marker-color preferences; and
- your consent choice, stored so the extension remembers that you agreed.

The extension uses this information only to save, display, edit, navigate to, and manage your bookmarks and preferences. It does not create an account, track browsing for advertising, or send this information to the developer or other third parties.

## Import and export

Import reads a JSON file only after you choose it. Export creates a JSON download only after you choose Export and confirm the selected videos. Exported files contain videos and bookmarks, not appearance or other preferences. You control where downloaded files are kept and whether you share them.

## Storage and deletion

Bookmark data and preferences stay in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. They are not synchronized to another device by this extension. You can delete saved data from the extension's interface, clear the extension's stored data in Chrome, or uninstall the extension. Imported and exported JSON files are separate files under your control.

## Sharing and security

The extension does not transmit the information described in this policy to the developer or third parties. Chrome and YouTube handle information under their own policies when you use those services. Protect any JSON backup you choose to export or share, as it contains your saved video IDs, titles, and timestamps.

Your use of information handled by this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The extension uses data only to provide its described bookmarking features; it does not sell data, use it for advertising, or permit humans to read it.
